Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Slide Studio Publisher ("the app") is used only by its owner with his own TikTok account.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the authorized account (open ID, display name, avatar).
  • Statistics of the authorized account and its videos (follower count, likes, views, comments count).
  • Videos uploaded by the owner through the app.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to analyse the performance of the owner's own content. Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data of other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings; local data is deleted on request or when no longer needed.
